House rules

Hot Dice

Roll six dice and keep at least one scoring die each roll: a one is 100, a five is 50, three alike are 100 times the number (three ones are 1,000), and each extra matching die doubles it. A straight or three pairs is 1,500. Roll the rest, or bank your turn. Roll nothing that scores and you bust and lose the turn. Score with all six and you roll all six again. The first past the target gives everyone one last turn.

Yacht

Roll five dice up to three times, holding any you like between rolls, then score them in one empty box on your card. Ones to Sixes count that number. Full house (three and two) and Choice score the total; four of a kind scores those four; a little straight (1 to 5) or big straight (2 to 6) is 30; five alike, a yacht, is 50. Twelve rounds; highest total wins.

Shut the Box

Roll two dice and flip down open tiles that add up to the roll. Keep going until you can't make the roll; the tiles still open are your score. Once the open tiles add up to six or less, you may roll one die. Shut every tile for a perfect zero. Lowest total wins.

Bluff

Everyone shakes their dice under a cup and looks only at their own. Take turns raising the bid: "at least this many dice, across every cup, show this face." Ones are wild. Raise the count, or keep it and pick a higher face. Instead of raising, call "Liar!" Every cup is lifted: if the bid was short, the bidder loses a die; if not, the caller does. Last cup standing wins.

Tap dice to keep or hold them, tap tiles to flip them. Keys: 1 to 9 pick dice or tiles, arrows and Enter use the buttons, Esc pauses.
