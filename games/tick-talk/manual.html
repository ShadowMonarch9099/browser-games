Tick Talk Defusal Manual

You have the rules. Your friend has the bomb. Do not look at their screen, and do not let them look at this. Ask them what they see, work out the answer here, and tell them what to do.

First, ask for the SERIAL. It looks like KE-472: two letters, then three digits. Most rules use it.

Fuse rack

Ask: the colours of the five fuses, left to right.

  1. Look at the colour of the first fuse (far left).
  2. Count how many of the five fuses are that colour, including the first one. Call it K.
  3. If the serial's letters include A, E or U: pull fuse number K, counting from the left.
  4. Otherwise: pull fuse number 6 − K.

Example: red, blue, red, white, green → K = 2. Serial KE-472 has an E, so pull fuse 2.

Glyph pad

Ask: which four symbols are on the buttons.

This is the Great Order:

◆ ☾ ▲ ♥ ✚ ★ ■ ♪ ● ☂ ⚑ ✿

  • Press the four symbols in the order they appear in the Great Order, left to right.
  • If the last digit of the serial is even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8): use the opposite order, right to left.

Names to say out loud: diamond, moon, triangle, heart, cross, star, square, note, circle, umbrella, flag, flower.

Compass dial

Ask: which of the three lamps are lit, left to right.

  1. A lit lamp is worth: left 4, middle 2, right 1. Add up the lit ones.
  2. Add the last digit of the serial.
  3. If the total is 8 or more, subtract 8. Repeat until it is below 8.
  4. Turn the dial to that direction and press Lock.

Code lock

Ask: the three-digit number on the display.

  1. Add the serial's three digits, as one number, to the number on the display.
  2. The code is the last three digits of the total. Press OK.

Example: display 615, serial KE-472 → 615 + 472 = 1087 → code 087.

Signal lamp

Ask: the three colours the lamp flashes, in order. There is a pause before it repeats.

Translate each colour, then press the translated colours in the same order.
