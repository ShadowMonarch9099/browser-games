How to play

  1. Split up. One player sits with this screen: the bomb. Everyone else opens the manual on their phones, and must not see the bomb.
  2. Talk. The bomb player describes what they see. The readers find the module in the manual and say what to do.
  3. The serial. Almost every rule depends on the serial number in the top corner. Read it out first.
  4. Mistakes. A wrong answer is a mistake. Three and it goes off. So does an empty clock.
  5. Swap. Next round, someone else holds the bomb.
