How to drive

  1. Steer the engine. The train follows its engine square by square. It never stops.
  2. Pick up passengers. Each one adds a carriage. Golden passengers are worth five, but they don't wait long.
  3. Don't crash. Fences, trees, cottages and carriages, including your own, end the run.
  4. Delivery: drive through a station and every carriage of its colour unloads. The more at once, the bigger the bonus, and the train gets shorter.
  5. Duel: last train running wins. Rivals: wrecked trains scatter their passengers.
