The shoreline

Settings

How to play

  1. Swap two creatures side by side to line up three or more of a kind. Drag, tap one then its neighbour, or use the keys.
  2. Every pool has a goal and a number of moves, shown at the top: pop certain creatures, wash away sand, crack barnacle stones or reach a score.
  3. Sand washes away when a match is made on top of it. Dark sand needs two washes. Stones crack when something pops beside them, and sink as things fall.
  4. Moves to spare turn into waves for a tide bonus, and earn more stars.
Wave4 in a line. Sweeps its row or column.
BubbleAn L or T shape. Bursts everything around it.
Pearl5 in a line. Swap with a creature to pop every one of its kind.

Swap two specials together for a bigger splash. Keys: arrows move, Enter picks up, then an arrow swaps. H for a hint, Esc to pause.
