House rules

  1. Pick up a tile whose face is bright. Dimmed tiles have another tile resting on them.
  2. Picked tiles go on the serving tray, next to any of their kind. Three alike are served and leave.
  3. Seven saucers. If the tray fills with no three alike, the table is lost.
  4. Clear the whole table to win. Every table can be cleared.
