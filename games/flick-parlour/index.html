How to flick

  1. Slide the striker along your baseline (the double line in front of you).
  2. Pull back and let go anywhere on the board: the striker flies the opposite way. The further you pull, the harder the flick.
  3. You play white. Sink a white coin (or the queen) and you flick again.
  4. The red queen must be covered: sink one of your coins on the same flick or the next, or she goes back to the middle.
  5. Pocketing the striker is a foul: one of your sunk coins comes back.
  6. Win by sinking all nine of your coins once the queen has been covered.

Keys: left and right slide the striker, A and D (or Z and X) turn the aim, up and down set the strength, Space flicks. Gamepad: d-pad slides, right stick aims, A flicks.
