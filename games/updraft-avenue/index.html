How to fly

  1. Roll and fly your kite that many roofs along the numbered path, from 1 up to 100.
  2. Updrafts (swirls of warm air) lift you from their start to their end, further up the hill.
  3. Drainpipes (copper pipes with a funnel) slide you from the funnel down to the spout.
  4. Land on another kite and it's blown back three roofs.
  5. Reach 100 exactly. Roll too high and you bounce back off the clocktower.

House rules: Pick a die: roll two and fly either. Sixes again: a six rolls once more (not a fourth time). Ribbons: the first kite to land on a ribbon roof keeps it, and can spend it to roll again.

Keys: R or Enter to roll, arrows and Enter to pick a die, Esc to pause. Touch: tap the dice to roll, or tap a glowing roof to fly there.
