How to climb

⤒Jump up through

Ledges let you jump up from below. A 2 m step is a plain jump.

⇡Boost

3 m is too high. Stand on your partner's head and jump.

⚓Anchor

Hold anchor and you can't be pulled. Up reels your partner in, down lets rope out.

▲Summit

Both of you must stand on the flag ledge.
