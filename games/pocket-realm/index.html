How to play

  1. Draw a land. Each turn you are offered two. Pick one, then set it on a glowing plot next to your hero, who walks onto it.
  2. Face what lives there. Forests hide wolves, ruins hold ghosts and gold, villages sell potions, lakes heal.
  3. Neighbours change lands. A village by a swamp becomes a witch's hut; a mountain by a lake, a waterfall; and more to find.
  4. Boxed in? Walk back over lands you have placed to reach open ground.
  5. The keep. After 26 lands the Dragon's Keep appears. Be ready.
