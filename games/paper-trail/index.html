How to play

  1. Draw from the dot. Drag, tap the next square, or use the arrow keys. The line must visit every square once.
  2. Holes are holes. The line can't cross a torn square.
  3. Stuck? Fold. Folding the sides makes the left and right edges touch, so the line can step from one to the other. Fold top and bottom for the other pair.
  4. Go back. Drag back over the last square, press undo, or tap any square on the line to erase to it.
  5. Stamps. Three for a page done without hints, two with one or two hints, one after that.
