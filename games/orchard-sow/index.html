How to sow

The near row of six bowls is yours, and so is the long store on your right. Pick up every seed in one of your bowls and drop them one at a time into the following bowls: right along your row, up past your store, then left along the far row.

Orchard rules

  • Seeds also drop into your store (never theirs).
  • Drop your last seed in your store and sow again.
  • Drop your last seed in one of your empty bowls and you take it and everything in the bowl opposite.
  • When either row runs out, each side keeps what is left on its own row. Most seeds in store wins.

Harvest rules

  • Stores hold only captured seeds. A lap of 12 or more skips the bowl you started from.
  • Drop your last seed in their bowl, making it 2 or 3, and you take it, plus the bowls before it that also hold 2 or 3.
  • A capture that would take every seed they have does not count, and if their row is empty you must sow seeds to them if you can.
  • First to more than half the seeds wins.

Tap a glowing bowl, or use the arrows and Enter (keys 1 to 6 pick a bowl). Esc pauses.
