Who is sailing?

Settings

How to sail

  1. Launch. Roll a 6 to sail a boat out of its harbour onto the river.
  2. Race round. Boats travel once round the river, clockwise, then up their own coloured lantern channel to the ghat in the middle.
  3. Sink a rival. Stop exactly on a lone rival boat and it goes back to its harbour. You roll again.
  4. Lamp buoys are safe. Squares with a floating lamp, and every harbour mouth, can't be attacked.
  5. Fast water. Stop on the arrows at a bend and the current carries you three squares on.
  6. Sixes. A 6 rolls again, but three 6s in a row ends the turn. Get all four boats home first to win.
